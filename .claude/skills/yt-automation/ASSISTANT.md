# YouTube pipeline: assistant checklist

The board is the Notion **YouTube Production Pipeline**. Your job is the human judgment at three points, about **45 minutes per video**. Everything else runs on its own.

## Every Monday (10 min): approve topics
1. Filter the board to **Status = Idea**.
2. For each channel, read the Angle and Evidence and open 1–2 evidence links.
3. Set **Approved** on the best 3 per channel (2 for a channel posting twice a week, plus one spare). Set **Rejected** on the rest, with a one-line reason in Notes. That teaches the Scout.

## When a card is "Scripted" (20–30 min): fact-check
1. Open the episode folder (path on the card) in GitHub, then open `FACTCHECK.md` and `sources.md`.
2. For each line, find the claim in a source. Correct? Tick it. Wrong or unsupported? Fix the sentence in `script.json` (or delete it), then tick it.
3. Anything about a living person or a company's wrongdoing needs **two** sources, or it comes out.
4. When everything is ticked: commit, then set the card to **Fact-checked**.

## When a card is "Rendered" (15–20 min): approve the video
Watch the Preview link (the private YouTube video or the preview file) at 1.5× speed. Check:
- [ ] Voice matches what's on screen, with no long frozen screens
- [ ] No wrong spelling of names, places or brands in captions or text
- [ ] Photos fit the story (no random or unrelated images, no logos as the main image, no real people made with AI)
- [ ] Music is quiet under the voice
- [ ] Both thumbnails readable at phone size; pick one (note A or B)
- [ ] Title and description make sense

All good: set **Approved to publish**, with your thumbnail choice in Notes. Something's off: put the timestamp and the problem in Notes and set the card back to **Fact-checked** (the Producer re-renders).

## Until YouTube approves our API (first few weeks)
API uploads land as **Private**. When the Publisher marks a card Scheduled, open YouTube Studio → Content and check the video shows the scheduled date. If it says Private with no date, set **Visibility → Schedule** to the card's Publish at time.

## Weekly (Friday, 5 min)
Read the Analyst report (path in the Friday summary). Add any idea it suggests as an **Idea** card.

## Never
- Approve a video you haven't fully watched.
- Publish anything showing a real person generated by AI.
- Share or paste the YouTube or Google keys anywhere.
