# Phases of the Moon (kids song, pilot)

Suno title: Phases of the Moon Song
Suno style: children's educational pop, cheerful clear female vocal, ukulele, glockenspiel, handclaps, soft kick drum, 110 bpm, bright, simple sing-along melody
Exclude styles: rap, autotune, heavy drums, dark

```
[Intro]
Look up, look up, at the sky tonight!
The moon is changing, shining bright!

[Verse 1]
The moon can't make its own light,
The sun shines on it, big and bright!
As the moon goes round and round,
We see new shapes from the ground!

[Chorus]
Phases, phases, phases of the moon!
It grows and it shrinks like a big balloon!
Eight shapes every month, so bright,
Phases of the moon at night!

[Verse 2]
New moon, dark, it hides from me,
Waxing crescent, a smile I see!
First quarter, half is bright,
Waxing gibbous, almost full tonight!
Waxing means it's growing, growing, growing!

[Chorus]
Phases, phases, phases of the moon!
It grows and it shrinks like a big balloon!
Eight shapes every month, so bright,
Phases of the moon at night!

[Verse 3]
Full moon, a big round ball,
Waning gibbous, getting small,
Third quarter, half once more,
Waning crescent, then dark like before!
Waning means it's shrinking, shrinking, shrinking!

[Bridge]
Can you say them with me? Let's go!
New moon, crescent, quarter, gibbous, full!
Gibbous, quarter, crescent, new!
Twenty-nine and a half days,
Then it starts all over for you!

[Final Chorus]
Phases, phases, phases of the moon!
It grows and it shrinks like a big balloon!
Eight shapes every month, so bright,
Phases of the moon at night!

[Outro]
See you tomorrow, moon!
```
